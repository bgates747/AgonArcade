# Both large-room positions sustain about 29 FPS through a full turn

The Author's E1L1 cells (34,33) and (34,36) each complete an exact360° clockwise turn at **29.0 completed-renderer FPS** on Oryx Flexe. Both retain the existing nominal30Hz simulation/submission cap. The southern point has slightly longer submission-to-callback delays in this sample, while the cap keeps average FPS effectively equal.

| (34,36), near south wall | 4.136100 s | 120 | 29.0 | 23.0 ms |

1. **Full-turn coverage.** Each interval starts at the first completed moving heading and ends when that same heading returns after exactly360°. All120three-degree headings are rendered, with no skipped angular steps and no player translation. Player centers are (34.5,33.5) and (34.5,36.5). Unlike the preceding starting-room test, these are one full turn each, rather than two30-second windows. The Author's full-turn request superseded that initial proposal.
2. **Timing and validation.** A host preload hook adds CLOCK_MONOTONIC timestamps to existing UART trace writes. It observes unchanged pose/render commands, delivered W3DR completions and C3 commands; no application or firmware code is instrumented. Every timed event matches independently decoded raw UART data. All measured completed frames are presented once. Both complete captures contain129completions/129C3commands; the center capture additionally ends with one unmeasured render request still in flight after the completed turn. The reported delay includes transport, queues, drawing and callback readout; it is not isolated VDP CPU time. Monitor scanout is not measured. [Machine-readable results](RESULTS.json), [analysis](../positions_analyze.py).
3. **Workload preservation.** Application, assets, firmware, MOS, Flexe runtime, host tracing/input shim and30Hz policy are identical between positions. Only the isolated medium-state player coordinates differ. Normal AI/combat remains enabled: the guard is active and health falls from100to94in each final scene, with no death/fizzle. [Center image](CENTER.png), [southern image](SOUTH.png). This is a capped gameplay comparison, not maximum unpaced renderer capacity or hardware performance.
4. **Preserved startup failure.** The first southern attempt stalled during loading, before any game render or timed pose/request event. Its1,422,695outbound bytes are an exact prefix of the successful center startup stream. The attempt timed out, stopped cleanly and restored boost. A fresh profile with byte-identical inputs completed the southern turn; both attempts remain under evidence. No emulator fix is claimed and the failed load contributes no FPS sample.
5. **Power and teardown.** Both SDL drivers were dummy throughout. Boost was temporarily0and restored to its original1after every attempt. Maximum sampled temperatures were56°C center,62°C during the failed startup, and57°C successful south. All profiles are stopped, input hashes are unchanged and backend fault counters are zero. Original Wolf source/generated/deployed files and prior benchmark evidence remain preserved. No physical deployment, source optimization or commit/push occurred.

The small FPS difference is not meaningful: both round to29.0and are constrained by the same scheduler. The observed latency difference is a single-turn sample; independent repeats or a separately released unpaced fixture would be needed to quantify spare capacity and a stable difference in rendering cost.
