# Returned native phase observations

The first two 128-frame diagnostics point toward drawing and display synchronization rather than simulation or road-coordinate arithmetic. Road and actor drawing account for about 77% of the quad variant's measured work after excluding its swap interval. These are raw emulator counter observations; the 900-frame completed/fenced comparisons remain the throughput evidence, and hardware remains the performance gate.

1. **The records pass their intended checks.** Both runs return all 128 frames / 512 simulation ticks, match the independent full final state, retain a stable reported PRID, and show positive counter progress. Nested construction counters fit inside their parent preparation interval. Tail and cross-frame intervals telescope without an observed wrap. The numerical source is [phase_ANALYSIS.json](phase_ANALYSIS.json), derived from `evidence/hzcandidate01/phase-control/` and `phase-float-quad-layered/`.
2. **Coordinate computation is a small share here.** Quad means are 99,026 raw counts for four physics entries and 46,741 for the complete preparation entry. Road-list construction contributes 29,557 within that preparation entry; it must not be added again. Physics plus preparation is about 4.39% of the recorded work excluding swap. This observation belongs to the native fixture and says nothing about the separate eZ80 full-game physics/projection cost.
3. **Road and actors are the first work targets.** Excluding swap, quad measured work averages 3,319,181 raw counts. Road dispatch/drawing/flush consumes 42.56%, actors 34.54%, clear/title 14.08%, and the player about 4.34%. These include command interpretation, rendering, flushes, marker overhead and any interruptions. The current span-to-bitmap road candidate addresses the largest measured drawing category. If that succeeds, actor transformation/plotting deserves its own controlled experiment. More coordinate-math specialization alone offers limited benefit in this measured native path.
4. **The quad road improvement is visible independently of swap.** Road-stage mean falls from 1,665,382 to 1,412,708 raw counts, about 15.17%. Total recorded work excluding swap falls about 7.54%. These opening 128 frames do not establish the same gain across the complete 900-frame workload or on hardware.
5. **Swap must remain its own category.** Its mean falls from 3,545,567 counts to 1,974,873, but its distribution changes sharply: 118 of 128 control intervals exceed two million counts, compared with 61 quad intervals; 67 quad intervals are below 500,000. This is consistent with different arrival phases at the display boundary. It is not evidence of a 44% faster rasterizer. The short diagnostic's total measured-work reduction is about 25.81%, much of it this changed swap interval. Keep that figure separate from headline throughput and useful drawing work.
6. **PRID 52685 is an intentional emulated core token.** The pinned Flexe source explicitly resets PRO_CPU/core0 PRID to `0xCDCD` and assigns core1 `0xABAB`; its spinlock owner constants use those same values. Thus `0xCDCD` is not an uninitialized-data diagnosis, a physical-chip identifier, or a clock calibration. Stable PRID supports the per-core comparison within this model. See pinned vendor `fbda8e905ccff36a090e4e33b0ffc2337dd79c52`, `src/xtensa.c` reset defaults, `src/flexe_session.c` core1 setup and `src/xtensa.h` spinlock constants; [source audit](phase_SOURCE.json).
7. **Calibration of one means counter coherence, not nanoseconds.** Both back-to-back read ranges are exactly one raw count. The pinned interpreter/JIT advances CCOUNT using abstract guest instruction accounting and corrects reads inside compiled instruction blocks. Together with positive bounded records, this supports comparative raw-counter attribution. It does not model every ESP32 instruction latency, cache/memory stall, or physical execution cycle. No ESP32 MHz conversion is applied. The usable PRT and nominal MOS loop durations still differ by roughly 3.5%; host runtime includes startup/readout/teardown. None of those clocks should be silently substituted for another.

The source boundary for the swap observation is controller swap execution followed by the ordinary stock drawing drain, before the final native marker. It does not observe completion of physical monitor scanout. The diagnostic's extra acknowledgments, flushes and counter instrumentation remain outside the headline fixture contract.
