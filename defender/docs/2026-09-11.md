   The user explained the software-sprite limitation and recommended ordinary
   bitmap plots for double buffering. The final renderer follows that advice;
   subsequent autoplay capture exited cleanly and showed single, clean objects.
6. Capture observes completed SDL frames using a temporary process-local hook
   in the ignored `.emulator/capture/` directory. It injects no game-state

   Fab's input path. Captured BMPs are converted losslessly to PNG with Pillow.

   resumed; M showed sound OFF; Escape restored the MOS prompt. The capture
